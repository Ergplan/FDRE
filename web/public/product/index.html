How it works

From RfS to bid in one flow

One story for every tender, with each step feeding the next and every number traceable to the clause it came from.

01 · READ

Read the tender

Upload the RfS and its amendments. Layout-aware parsing with table recognition and OCR pulls out the requirements, deadlines, securities, penalties and risks.

02 · CONFIRM

Confirm the requirements

Every value shows its confidence, page and clause. Edit or lock anything; locked values survive a corrigendum.

03 · OPTIMIZE

Size and price

Solar, wind and battery sizes, oversizing and augmentation are optimized against the full-term tariff, with degradation, debt, tax and penalties included.

04 · BID

Bid and advise

The tariff or capacity charge at your target IRR, the margin to the ceiling, a compliance checklist, the financial model and saved, versioned scenarios for the bid committee.

Round the Clock

The cheapest plant that meets the DFR every year

A HiGHS linear programme finds the global optimum of the 25-year tariff, and the exact financial model then prices its neighbours. You see the answer and the proof.

  • A proven floor: no design in range can beat the stated tariff
  • Tariff map across every solar × wind size
  • What ageing costs: degradation and augmentation, in ₹/kWh
  • Shadow price of DFR: what each extra point would add to the tariff
BESS Tender

From the RfS to the capacity charge

Upload a storage tender and confirm what it asks for. Joulewise then prices every oversizing and augmentation strategy over the contract term and returns the lowest compliant capacity charge.

  • Requirements with confidence, page and clause
  • Energy at the delivery point kept every year, block by block
  • Bid against the ceiling, with VGF and penalties priced in
  • Every strategy on one map; click to load any of them
Tender intelligence

Every number traceable to its clause

Layout-aware parsing (Docling) reads tables and scanned pages; clause-aware retrieval finds the requirements and risks. Nothing is a black box: open any value and read the tender text behind it.

  • Penalties, securities, bid limits and timelines
  • Risk flags with severity and bid implication
  • Amendments tracked against the base tender
Dispatch and audit

See every hour, and every decision

Day, month and year dispatch, including surplus charging the battery, curtailment and its reasons. The optimizer log records what was searched and why the answer won.

  • Hour-by-hour flows and state of charge
  • Solver log with HiGHS output, downloadable
  • Saved scenarios with version history and comparison
