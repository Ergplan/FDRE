How it works

From RfS to bid in one flow

One story for every tender, with each step feeding the next and every number traceable to the clause it came from.

01 · READ

Read the tender

Upload the RfS and its amendments. Layout-aware parsing with table recognition and OCR pulls out the requirements, deadlines, securities, penalties and risks.

02 · CONFIRM

Confirm the requirements

Every value shows its confidence, page and clause. Edit or lock anything; locked values survive a corrigendum.

03 · OPTIMIZE

Size and price

Solar, wind and battery sizes, oversizing and augmentation are optimized against the full-term tariff, with degradation, debt, tax and penalties included.

04 · BID

Bid and advise

The tariff or capacity charge at your target IRR, the margin to the ceiling, a compliance checklist, the financial model and saved, versioned scenarios for the bid committee.

Data is encrypted to the same standards banks and governments rely on: AES-256 for stored data, databases and backups, and TLS 1.3 for every connection between your browser and the platform.
