Scope: RJ-200 project ▾claude-sonnet-5 · 12.4k tokens today
Can we bank unscheduled peak-hour energy against next month's availability test?
No. The availability test is monthly and standalone — clause 5.4 states shortfall in any month attracts LD "notwithstanding surplus supply in any other month" RFS.pdf p.36 §5.4. Energy offered but unscheduled may be sold to third parties RFS.pdf p.38 §6.2, but it earns no compliance credit. The engine models this the same way (no inter-month banking).
Explain why run #409 was infeasible.
Run #409 (SJVN A1) failed hard compliance: year-1 peak availability reached only 87.2% vs the 90% floor. Binding constraint: BESS power (60 MW) covers just 60% of the 100 MW contracted capacity in zero-RE evening hours; July has 9 such days. Options: raise BESS power ≥ 90 MW, or enable green support (covers ≈1.1% of the 2.8% gap — insufficient alone) run #409 · compliance.
